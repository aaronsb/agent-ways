# Signs of Generated Writing

When the user asks for a draft to read less like a model wrote it, work from the lists below. The user sends the draft under their own name. The lists draw on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" page.

## Root rule

Replace each claim of importance with the name, number, date or result behind it. Use only facts you were given or checked. Do not work out new dates, weekdays, totals or promises. If no fact stands behind the claim, delete it.

## Rewrite when they cluster

One word can be chance. Several in one draft are the sign.

| Kind | Examples | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Stock words | additionally (as an opener), crucial, pivotal, testament, showcase, underscore, highlight (verb), fostering, vibrant, multifaceted, enduring, delve | The plain word, or the fact |
| Stand-ins for is or has | serves as, stands as, functions as, boasts, features (meaning has) | is, has |
| Brochure and legacy filler | rich history, renowned, groundbreaking, commitment to, plays a key role, evolving landscape, sets the stage for, reflects broader trends | The fact, or cut |
| Trailing -ing clause | ", highlighting its importance", ", ensuring", ", fostering" | End the sentence at the fact |
| Formula | "Despite its strengths, X faces challenges", "continues to thrive", a triad for rhythm, "Y rather than X" when nobody said X | Name the risk with its owner and next step, or cut |
| Chatbot framing | "Certainly!", "Here is the revised version:", "I hope this helps", "Would you like me to", "In conclusion" | Delete |
| Clerk talk | "I am writing to", "I understand your concern", "I assure you", "I apologize for any inconvenience", "please don't hesitate" | The action and date the user gave, or cut |
| Edit notes | "refined for clarity and flow", "while preserving the original meaning" | Delete |
| Open asks | "I welcome any feedback", "If there are specific areas" | One specific question |

## Shapes that give it away

- A recap closing every section, the same three-bullet shape repeated, a roadmap line ("In this section we will"), or an opening line that defines the document. Open with the finding and cut the recap.
- Bold on a key phrase in every sentence.
- Every source or item given the identical treatment. Say what differs between them.
- In a short message: headings, a pasted "Subject:" line, stacked closers, and a closing recap. Stop once the ask is made.

## Keep what is natural

- Keep plain is, are and has, a true "the only" or "the first", and honest qualifiers such as "perhaps". Correct grammar is no sign.
- The user's own text and sent mail outrank these lists. A phrase they use stays.
- Leave quotes and quoted drafts word for word.

## Self-edit pass

1. Delete offers and notes about your edits.
2. Keep one name for each thing.
3. Check each name, number, link and quote against its source (writing/provenance).
4. Cut each sentence the reader would not miss.

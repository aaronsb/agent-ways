# Signs of generic writing

The user sends these drafts under their own name, so cut what makes text generic. The list follows Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" page.

## Root rule

Replace each claim of importance with the name, number, date, or result behind it. Use only facts you were given or checked. Do not work out new dates, weekdays, totals, or promises. If there is no fact behind the claim, delete it.

## Rewrite when they cluster

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| Words | additionally (as an opener), crucial, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, fostering, highlight (verb), pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant, multifaceted, quietly |
| For is or has | serves as, stands as, functions as, holds the distinction of, boasts or features meaning has |
| Stiff verbs | utilized, authored, relocated, attempted |
| Filler | a triad for rhythm, a trailing clause like "highlighting its importance", "Y rather than X" when nobody said X |
| Leftovers | "Here is the revised version:", "In conclusion", [Name], oaicite |

One word can be chance. Several make a draft generic.

## Keep natural phrasing

- Use plain is, are, and has. Keep a true "the only" or "the first".
- Keep honest qualifiers such as "perhaps". Perfect grammar is not a sign.
- Follow the user's own text and sent mail over this list.

## Vary the structure

Text with only short declarative sentences reads flat. In paragraphs, give the reason in the same sentence with because, so, or when. In bullets and short messages, write one idea per sentence.

## Self-edit pass

1. In the draft, delete offers ("Would you like me to") and notes about your edits.
2. Keep one name for each thing.
3. Check each name, number, link, and quote against its source.
4. Cut each sentence the reader would not miss, including ones that only show your care.

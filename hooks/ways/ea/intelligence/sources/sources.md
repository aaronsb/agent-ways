# Meeting Prep Sources

Beyond the event, mail, chat, files and tasks, prep draws on:

- **Business records.** The CRM account and open deals, and any wiki or project pages tied to the topic.
- **The attendees themselves.** Look up each person in internal records and their public professional profile, so the user knows their role and history.
- **Commitments in the record.** Promises made in email, chat, or past transcripts that this meeting may test.

For the previous occurrence, read the transcript, not the AI summary. Summaries drop the exact words and who said them.

When you cannot see some context, such as a private side thread, say so and ask.
